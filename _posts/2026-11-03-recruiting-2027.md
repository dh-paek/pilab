PILab is recruiting **graduate students (M.S., Ph.D., integrated M.S.–Ph.D.) and undergraduate researchers for the 2027 academic year**.

- Topics: 4D radar perception, multi-modal sensor fusion, 3D occupancy, multi-object tracking, efficient and neuromorphic embedded AI
- How to apply: email your CV, transcript, and a short statement (3–5 sentences) on your research interests.
